# Jumptastix
My Haven Delhi Game on the topic bounce back 
Welcome to the game! Here is everything you need to know to jump in, stay alive, hit the target score, and recover from a fall without guessing a single control.1. Objective & Win ConditionGoal: Build up your jump score and reach the Win Score of 20 (jump_score >= 20).Scoring: Every normal jump and every trampoline bounce increases your score (+1).Failure / Death: Falling onto the ground when you aren't safely landing/bouncing or dropping below the bottom boundary triggers your fall sound (Sound4), saves your run's score (global.last_jump_score), and sends you to the death screen.2. ControlsActionInputHow It WorksMove Left / RightA / DMoves Aryan horizontally left (A) or right (D). Speed scales dynamically as your progress and run time increase.JumpSpace or WJumps upward when on solid ground. Grants +1 to your score and triggers the jump sound (snd_aryan_jump).Aim GrenadeHold Left Mouse Button & DragPulls back like a slingshot to set the launch trajectory and shows yellow trajectory preview dots ahead of Aryan.Throw GrenadeRelease Left Mouse ButtonLaunches the grenade along the aimed arc with an orange trail behind it.Respawn / RetryR or Click Respawn ButtonResets the run from the death screen so you can jump right back into action cleanly.3. Gameplay Mechanics & TipsJumps, Gravity, & Apex SlowdownAs you climb and near the peak of a jump (abs(vsp) becomes small while rising), a brief apex slowdown kicks in for a few frames, giving you extra breathing room to line up your next move or aim a throw.The world scrolls smoothly as you push forward or as speed tiers ramp up over distance and time.TrampolinesLanding on a trampoline compresses it (squash_y) and launches you much higher than a standard jump while adding +1 to your score.Trampolines are automatically cleared when entering the death screen and during respawns so nothing lingers on the death page.Grenades (Greande)Holding Left Click and dragging aims your shot; releasing throws the grenade with gravity pulling it down along a curved path and leaving an orange trail.Grenades can even bounce upward off trampolines during flight (vsp rebounds and hsp gets a slight boost) before hitting the ground or expiring after their timer (alarm[0]).Once thrown, you'll need to recharge/regain your grenade on the ground before throwing again.Death Screen & Score TrackingYour final score from that attempt is stored in global.last_jump_score and displayed on the death screen (SCORE: <value>).Hit R or click the respawn button to start a fresh attempt.4. Quick Beginner ChecklistUse A and D to position yourself and Space / W to time jumps.Watch the yellow preview dots while holding Left Click before releasing a grenade.Chain jumps and trampoline bounces toward 20 points to win.If you fall into the death screen, check your score and press R (or click Respawn) to try again.
